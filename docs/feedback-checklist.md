# Feedback checklist

After a real-use session, review the stored feedback signals by exercise,
attempt, session, pattern, scene, difficulty, provider, model, verdict, and
evaluation scores. Check whether `too_easy` and `too_hard` agree with the
difficulty trace, whether `unnatural` clusters by generator/pattern, whether
`evaluation_inaccurate` clusters by provider/model/verdict, and whether
`repetitive` aligns with exact-repeat or streak flags.

Feedback is an observation channel only. It is not allowed to mutate mastery,
state, global difficulty, review timing, or probe budget directly.

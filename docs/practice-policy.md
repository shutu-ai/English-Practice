# Practice Policy

The candidate pool combines current productive zone (40%), weak skills (25%), scheduled reviews (15%), maintenance (10%), and new/probe work (10%). Candidate score adds difficulty fit, weakness, review urgency, graph readiness, and scene diversity, then subtracts recent pattern repetition.

The policy rejects the immediately previous pattern and patterns that exceed the configured recent window. Modes (`adaptive`, `weak`, `review`, and `assessment`) reuse the same selector and differ only in weights and eligibility.
